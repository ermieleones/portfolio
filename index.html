Validating 10,000+ AI-extracted invoice lines a month

Freight Data Validator, Expedock Software Inc. (2023 to present)

Problem

An AI system extracts invoice data quickly, but a wrong description, quantity or value on a customs entry can lead to incorrect duties, holds or penalties.

My role

Final human check on AI-extracted data before customs entry lodgment, at 10,000+ lines a month.

Approach

  1. Compare extracted fields with the source invoice: description, quantity, value, currency, shipment details.
  2. Test the logic: quantity × price equals line total, line totals equal invoice total.
  3. Use customs knowledge to flag descriptions too vague to classify.
  4. Correct clear errors, query the source when the document is ambiguous.

Result

98%+ accuracy at high volume, and data that reached lodgment already verified. Broker knowledge caught issues a non-specialist might miss.

Freight invoice validation before Accounts Payable

Freight Data Validator, Expedock Software Inc.

Problem

Freight invoices can contain duplicated charges, wrong shipment references or unsupported amounts. Once paid, mistakes are slow and costly to recover.

My role

Review 500+ freight invoices a month before they enter Accounts Payable.

Approach

  1. Match the invoice reference to the actual shipment record.
  2. Review each charge against supporting documents.
  3. Check quantities and units against shipment data.
  4. Release clean invoices to AP, return flagged ones with clear notes.

Result

500+ invoices a month checked before payment, giving AP a cleaner, verified input.

Tariff classification and duty and tax computation

Licensed Customs Broker, Independent Practice (2017 to 2023)

Problem

Every import declaration depends on correct classification and correct duty and tax figures. Errors mean penalties or overpayment.

My role

Classification, computation and consultation on import, export and PEZA shipments for 2 active clients.

Approach

  1. Review invoice, packing list and bill of lading to understand the goods.
  2. Classify under the correct tariff heading.
  3. Establish the dutiable value and apply the duty rate.
  4. Compute duties and taxes, then double-check.
  5. Advise the client on required documents before arrival.

Simplified fictional example: CIF PHP 1,000,000 + duty at 10% (100,000) + VAT at 12% on 1,100,000 (132,000) = PHP 232,000 total duties and taxes.

Result

Accurate, compliant assessments and practical guidance for clients on import, export and PEZA requirements.

MBL/HBL manifest verification

Import Specialist, Kintetsu Worldwide Express Philippines (2013)

Problem

In consolidated freight, house bills must agree with the master bill and manifest. Mismatches delay cargo at clearance.

My role

Process and verify 80+ cargo manifests a month against MBL and HBL.

Approach

  1. Compare consignee, packages, weights and descriptions across all documents.
  2. Document each discrepancy found.
  3. Coordinate corrections with customers, carriers and overseas offices.
  4. Monitor arrivals and confirm documents are ready for processing.

Result

Accurate, complete manifest data, with discrepancies corrected before they held up clearance.
